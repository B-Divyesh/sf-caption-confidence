How it works / 90 seconds

Add a layer of attention.
Nothing more.

Caption Confidence improves a track you already have. It does not transcribe audio, scrape protected streams, or make medical claims.

  1. 01
    WEBVTT

    Bring captions

    Import a local VTT or SRT file, or read a caption track that an HTML5 video exposes.

  2. 02
    SIP/SHIP

    Name your pairs

    Add the exact words you tend to confuse. Matching words receive a high-contrast lichen mark.

  3. 03
    R−0.8 s

    Replay once

    Press R from the page to return to the start of the current caption, with your chosen lead-in.

Download extension ZIP
  1. Unzip the download.
