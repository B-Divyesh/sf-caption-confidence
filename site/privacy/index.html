Read the privacy policy

Caption Confidence handles caption text on your device.

Caption files and tracks

The extension reads a VTT or SRT file in the active tab. It can also read a caption track exposed by that page.

It sends no caption text to Caption Confidence, Sociobot, or an analytics service.

Extension storage

Word pairs, text size, replay lead-in, and appearance settings use local extension storage.

The extension requests no browsing-history, microphone, camera, audio-capture, or video-capture permission.

Demo storage

The demo uses a session key beginning with demo:caption-confidence:. It does not read or change extension settings or website license data.

Imported demo files remain in the open tab and are discarded when you leave or reset the demo.

Purchases

Sociobot and Dodo process the optional purchase. They handle payment details, receipts, taxes, and eligible refunds.

License verification sends the pasted token to Sociobot. A timestamped result prevents another check for one day.

Website requests

The site has no advertising tracker or third-party analytics script. A service worker caches public site files for offline reloads.

Delete local data

Uninstall the extension to remove extension storage. Clear this site’s data to remove its license cache.

Email privacy@sociobot.in with privacy questions.
